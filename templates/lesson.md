# Replace with a concrete learner task

Authoring template: keep unpublished until reviewed. For a Zynq lesson, update the
parent, module ID, and permalink. This template directory is excluded from the build.

## Your task

Describe one useful outcome and the learner's starting point in two or three sentences.
State the simulation's limits and any tool or board assumptions.

## What you need to know

Introduce only the concepts required to make the first prediction.

## Predict

Ask what will change and why before revealing the result.

## Try one action

Provide one operation with a keyboard alternative and clear initial state.

## Inspect the change

Show the before and after states. Explain the reason, including what remains stale
or unaffected. Provide useful feedback for a likely incorrect prediction.

## Try a new situation

Change one relevant condition. Ask for an explanation without reusing the first answer.

## Check your understanding

Describe observable evidence of independent understanding. Keep activity completion
separate from mastery and allow unknown when no evidence is available.

## Sources and verification

Link primary references. State versions, synthetic data, simplified dependencies,
and the actual scope of any practical verification.
